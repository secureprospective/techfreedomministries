## LinkedIn — Company Overview (1848 / 2000)

Tech Freedom Ministries is a faith-backed nonprofit teaching ordinary people to escape Big Tech surveillance — and truly own the technology they depend on.

Digital sovereignty is a stewardship question, not a consumer one. Your phone, your laptop, even your car report on you to companies you've never met. We believe the tools you rely on should answer to you, and no one else.

THE MODEL
We run free Install Parties — three hours, a real machine, someone beside you the whole way. Bring a laptop (or use a donated one) and walk out owning it: Linux installed, your data yours, nobody with root but you. No cost. No catch.

From there, every participant follows a four-level Roadmap — Exodus, Catechism, Homestead, Great Commission — moving from a first install to self-hosting their own data, and finally to teaching others. Graduates become Vanguards who run events in their own cities.

HARDWARE IS THE MISSION
What Big Tech calls end-of-life, we call a first computer. Donated laptops, workstations, and mini PCs — devices written off by their owners — become someone's gateway to digital freedom. Hardware is our primary need.

THE VISION
A self-replicating movement: enough trained Vanguards in enough cities that digital freedom spreads without a head office pushing it. Every event seeds the next. Every graduate becomes a teacher. The rural church that could never afford a tech ministry now has one.

WHO WE SERVE
Individuals reclaiming their privacy. Churches and community groups hosting events. Donors and technologists who believe private, independent computing is a right, not a luxury.

Faith is our backbone. The door is open to everyone — any background, any belief, any skill level.

"It is for freedom that Christ has set us free." — Galatians 5:1

Donate hardware, host an event, or partner with us: techfreedomministries.org
