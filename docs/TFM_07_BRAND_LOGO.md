## The Logo — What It Is

A hill viewed from the side. The hill is Golgotha — the place of humiliation and defeat. The surface of the hill is a circuit board — traces, pathways, the material of the digital cage. A plain Christian cross stands upright at the top, solid, permanent, unaffected by what surrounds it. The right side of the hill evaporates — breaking into pixels that scatter and fade into the dark background. The left side remains intact.

This is not a tech logo with a cross added. It is a theological statement rendered visually.

---

## The Meaning — Non-Negotiable

- The hill is the cage. The digital prison humans live inside without knowing it.
- The cross is Truth. It does not fight the cage. It rises from it unchanged.
- The dissolution is not an explosion or a battle. It is evaporation. The cage loses coherence in the presence of what stands above it.
- The cross does not conquer the circuit board. It makes the circuit board irrelevant.

This meaning must survive every derivative, every animation, every resized variant.

---

## The Narrative Moment

The viewer arrives mid-event. The cross has already risen. The cage is already losing. The outcome is not in question. The only thing left to witness is the completion.

This is not a before. This is not an after. This is the moment of already.

---

## Composition Rules

**Left to right narrative read — humans read left to right.**

| Element | Position | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Hill (cage) | Left and center base | Intact on the left, dissolving on the right |
| Cross | Center, top | Upright, solid, tallest element in the frame |
| Dissolution | Right side | Evaporation already well underway — not just beginning |

---

## What Must Never Change

- Cross is vertical and upright — never tilted, broken, or in distress
- Cross is visually distinct from the hill — different substance, not just different color
- Dissolution is on the right side only
- The cage is already losing — dissolution must be clearly underway, not just starting
- No battle imagery — no fire, swords, conflict, or violence
- Cross is the dominant vertical element — nothing equals or exceeds its visual weight
- No text embedded in the logo mark itself

---

## What Is Open for Refinement

- Color palette — teal/cyan is the current working direction, not permanently locked
- Circuit board density and detail on the hill surface
- How far the dissolution has progressed
- Pixel fragment size — smaller reads ethereal, larger reads deliberate
- Whether the cross base is embedded in the hill or rises above it
- Background treatment — pure black, deep navy, or transparent for placement flexibility

## Iteration History

### Session 1 — May 2026

**Original concept:** Approved as working base. Cross is solid. Hill has mass and weight. Dissolution reads correctly.

**NanoBanana Text to Image test:** Generated a comparison version. Technically competent but the cross became an outline — same visual material as the hill. Lost the theological distinction. The cage and Truth must not be made of the same stuff. Rejected as base.

**What the generated version got right:** Pixel dissolution direction was cleaner. Smaller fragments fading into nothing rather than a sharp edge. File this as a reference for dissolution refinement when returning to NanoBanana Image to Image.

**Locked decision:** Original logo is the base. Work forward from it, not from regeneration.
