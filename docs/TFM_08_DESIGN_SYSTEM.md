# TFM_08 — DESIGN SYSTEM
*The visual identity. Everything built for TFM — website, print, credentials, flyers — draws from this document.*

---

## Guiding Principle

This design system is built to last. Not to trend. The choices here should feel as correct in 2045 as they do today. When in doubt, ask: does this feel earned, or does it feel designed? TFM materials should feel earned.

## Typography — The Proclamation

Three tiers. Each tier has a fixed role. They do not swap.

### Tier 1 — Eyebrow and Navigation
- Font: System sans-serif (Inter, or system default)
- Size: 10–11px
- Weight: 400
- Letter spacing: 0.18–0.22em
- Case: ALL CAPS
- Color: Muted Gold `#8B7355`
- Use: Section labels above headlines, navigation links, badge labels, card subtitles

### Tier 2 — Headlines
- Font: Georgia (serif)
- Size: 32–42px (scale to context)
- Weight: 700 for the first phrase, 400 italic for the second phrase
- Color: Near-Black `#1C1209` for the strong word, Deep Gold `#8B6914` for the italic phrase
- Pattern: *Strong word or phrase* + *italic gold phrase*
- Example: **Break from the Grid.** *Own Your Machine.*
- Use: Page heroes, section headers, Roadmap card headlines, event signage

### Tier 3 — Body
- Font: System sans-serif
- Size: 14–16px
- Weight: 400
- Line height: 1.65
- Letter spacing: 0.01em
- Color: Warm Brown `#4A3F2F`
- Use: All paragraph text, descriptions, instructions, event copy

## Dividers and Decorative Elements

### The Diamond ◆
- Character: ◆ (U+25C6)
- Color: Bright Gold `#C4A84A`
- Size: 10–12px
- Use: Section breaks between headline and body, between nav items, on the Roadmap card between levels
- Never stack more than one diamond at a time

### The Rule
- Style: 1px solid horizontal line
- Color: Bright Gold `#C4A84A`
- Width: 40px (short accent rule) or full-width (section divider)
- Use: Below eyebrow text, between major page sections

### Corner Brackets
- Style: Single L-shaped lines at page or card corners
- Color: Bright Gold `#C4A84A`
- Weight: 1px
- Use: Formal print materials (credentials, Roadmap card). Do not use on every element — reserve for pieces that are meant to feel like documents.
- Rule: Classy, not repeated. One application per material, not per section.

## The Logo

### Format Rule
The logo is format-neutral. It works in any color environment.

| Context | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Parchment background | Logo in near-black `#1C1209` |
| White background | Logo in near-black `#1C1209` |
| Dark background (event signage, stickers) | Logo in white or parchment |
| Single-color print (flyers, stamps) | Logo in black only |
| Emboss / foil | Logo outline only — no fill needed |

### What Never Changes
- Cross is vertical and upright
- Dissolution is on the right side only
- The cage is already losing — dissolution is underway, not starting
- No battle imagery
- No text embedded in the logo mark itself
- The cross is the dominant vertical element

### What the Logo Is Not
The logo is not a tech logo with a cross added. It is a theological statement rendered visually. Every application of the logo should preserve that meaning.

## Physical Print Materials

### The Roadmap Card
- Size: 4" x 6" (not business card — this is meant to be kept)
- Stock: 100lb+ cardstock, waterproof coating preferred
- Front: Four-level layout with checkboxes, level names, level color badges
- Back: TFM logo + Galatians 5:1 + QR code linking to Events page
- Typography: Proclamation system throughout
- Corner brackets: Yes — this is a credential-adjacent document

### The Vanguard Credential Card
- Size: Standard credit card (3.375" x 2.125")
- Stock: Heavy — ideally matte laminate or linen finish
- Front: Vanguard name, city, TFM logo, "Vanguard" in eyebrow caps
- Back: Galatians 5:1 in italic Georgia, thin gold rule
- Corner brackets: Yes
- Level color: None — Vanguard credential is above the level system, uses gold accent only

### Event Flyers
- Background: White (not parchment — for print cost and photocopy compatibility)
- Logo: Near-black on white
- Typography: Proclamation system
- Color use: Limit to one level color if the flyer promotes a specific workshop track. Otherwise gold accents only.
- No corner brackets on flyers — reserve those for earned documents

## Voice and Copy Alongside the Design

The design system and the copy system are inseparable. A beautifully typeset flyer with corporate nonprofit language is still a broken flyer.

### Rules that apply in every context

- Short sentences. Vary length deliberately.
- No "stakeholders," "deliverables," "ecosystem," or "leverage."
- Layer 1 (faith context): lead with stewardship and sovereignty. Galatians 5:1 is appropriate.
- Layer 2 (secular/public context): lead with surveillance and ownership. No scripture in the hero.
- Layer 3 (member context): full TFM identity — levels, Vanguard, the Oath, the community.

### The Two-Phrase Headline Pattern

Every major headline follows the same typographic pattern:

**Strong declarative phrase** + *italic gold question or payoff phrase*

Examples:
- **Break from the Digital Grid.** *Own Your Machine.*
- **From Digital Subject** *to Digital Steward.*
- **You already know how to fix this.** *Most people don't.*
- **This event cost you nothing.** *Help us reach the next city.*
