## Mission Statement

People cannot escape Big Tech surveillance without hands-on training and independent hardware. With faith as our backbone and an open door for everyone, Tech Freedom Ministries runs free Install Parties where people walk out owning a laptop that is actually theirs — at zero cost. Every student follows a four-level Roadmap; the ones who go all the way become Vanguards — teachers who take the mission to their own city and run their own events. **We give freedom with a fishing pole because Christ has set us free;** *what Big Tech calls end-of-life, we call a first computer.*

**Tagline:** Giving freedom with a fishing pole.

## The Core Thesis

TFM is not a Linux club. It is a freedom movement with a repeating engine — fueled by the most reliable force in human psychology: the feeling of winning something that felt impossible, then wanting someone else to feel it.

The Install Party is the spark. The Roadmap is the ladder. The Vanguard is the payoff. Every person who completes the journey becomes the next event host. That is the viral loop.

## Legal Structure

- **Entity type:** 501(c)(3) Religious/Educational Organization
- **Filing form:** IRS 1023-EZ (for orgs projecting under $50K year one)
- **Requirements:** 3 board members, purpose statement, Articles of Incorporation (home state)
- **Texas filing cost:** ~$275 total
- **Priority:** File before accepting significant donations. EIN goes in footer of every church outreach email.
