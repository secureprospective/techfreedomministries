# TFM_05 — WEBSITE ARCHITECTURE & COPY DIRECTION
*What to build, in what order, and exactly what each page needs to say.*

For visual and typographic rules, see TFM_08_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md.
For technical build spec, see TFM_09_WEBSITE_BUILD.md.

---

## Build Sequence

Build these pages in this order. Do not skip ahead. An empty blog kills credibility faster than no blog.

1. Home
2. Events
3. Roadmap
4. Vanguard
5. Donate

Add a blog, forum, merch store, or podcast page only when you have content to fill them.

---

## Page 1 — HOME

### One Job
Stop the scroll. Make the visitor feel seen in the first 3 seconds.

### Hero Section
**Headline (pick one or A/B test):**
- *"You are the product. We'll show you how to stop."*
- *"Big Tech is watching. We teach people to walk away — for free."*
- *"Your computer is working against you. We can fix that."*

**Subhead (one sentence):**
> Tech Freedom Ministries teaches anyone — regardless of background — to own their digital life. Free events. Real tools. No catch.

**CTA button:** "Find an Event Near You" → links to Events page

**Secondary CTA:** "See How It Works" → scrolls to the explainer section below

---

### Video Block
90-second video. Shot on a phone is fine — authentic beats produced here. Show:
- What the room looks like at an Install Party (not staged — real people, real machines)
- One moment where something works and someone reacts
- The founder talking directly to camera for 20 seconds about why this exists

No voiceover. No music. No titles. Just the room.

---

### The Explainer Section (Below the Fold)
Three steps, visual icons, plain language:

**Step 1 — Come to an Install Party**
Bring your laptop. We'll replace your surveillance-ware with a system you own. Free. No experience needed.

**Step 2 — Follow the Roadmap**
A four-level journey from first-time Linux user to the person teaching others. At your pace. With community support.

**Step 3 — Become a Vanguard**
When you've got the skills, come back and help the next person. That's how TFM grows — not from the top down, but from graduate to graduate.

---

### Donation CTA (Bottom of Home Page)
Not a charity ask. A joining statement.

> *"Every event we run is free. Every tool we teach is free. If that's worth something to you — help us reach the next city."*

**[Give Now]** button → Donate page

---

## Page 2 — EVENTS

### One Job
Get someone from "interested" to "showing up" in under 60 seconds. This page must work perfectly on mobile.

### What It Contains
- Upcoming events listed chronologically
- For each event: date, time, city, venue name (not full address until RSVP confirmed for safety), what to bring (3 bullet points), RSVP button
- A "No events near you?" section with an email capture: *"Tell us your city — we'll let you know when TFM is coming."*
- A note for churches and venues: *"Want to host an event? It costs you nothing but a room."* → link to a simple contact form

---

## Page 3 — THE ROADMAP

### One Job
Make the four-level journey feel achievable and real. Visitors should be able to picture themselves at Level 4 within the first 30 seconds.

### Structure
Visual progression — not a wall of text. Each level gets:
- A color (green / yellow / red / white matching the physical card)
- A name and subtitle
- 5 milestones listed plainly
- What you unlock at completion

Include a callout box:

> *"The Roadmap isn't a course. There's no deadline. You move at your pace, with your community behind you. The only rule: when you finish, you come back and help someone else start."*

---

## Page 4 — VANGUARD

### One Job
Recruit technically-skilled people who have been looking for exactly this without knowing it existed.

### Hero Copy
> *"You already know how to fix this stuff. Most people don't."*
> *Tech Freedom Ministries is what happens when that knowledge gets aimed at something that matters.*

### What the Page Covers
- What a Vanguard is (one paragraph, plain language)
- What is expected: minimum 2 events per year, Field Manual completion
- What they get: credential card, community, expansion opportunity, their name on the cards they hand out
- Application form: name, city, email, a single question ("What's your Linux experience level?")

### Grant Credibility Note
This page passively functions as a credibility signal to grant reviewers. Keep a running count of active Vanguards and cities visible on this page once numbers are meaningful.

---

## Page 5 — DONATE

### One Job
Convert a person who already believes in TFM into a financial supporter. This page is not for convincing. The rest of the site did that. This page just needs to make giving easy and satisfying.

### Structure
One headline:
> *"Help us reach the next city."*

Three giving tiers, plain language impact statements:

| Amount | What It Does |
|---|---|
| $25 | Puts a USB drive in someone's hand |
| $100 | Funds one event's materials |
| $500 | Sponsors a full event for a new city |

One donate button (Stripe or PayPal Giving Fund — both work with 501(c)(3) status).

One short paragraph below the button:
> *Tech Freedom Ministries is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Your donation is tax-deductible. We don't sell data. We don't run ads. We teach people to protect themselves from the people who do.*

---

## Photography and Video Guidelines

- Real people at real events. Stock photos of computers are the fastest way to look like every other tech nonprofit.
- Mobile-first: Events page especially. RSVP flow must work on a phone in under 4 taps.
- No autoplay video on the home page. Embed the video so it plays on click.

---

## What NOT to Build Yet

- Blog (no content to fill it)
- Forum (no community size to sustain it)
- Merch store (no brand recognition to sell to)
- Podcast page (no episodes)
- Member portal (no CRM budget)

Build the five pages. Get the first event on the calendar. Then add.
